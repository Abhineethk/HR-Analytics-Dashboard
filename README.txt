# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition using Python, SQL, and Power BI. The objective is to identify the key factors contributing to employee turnover and provide actionable insights for HR decision-making.

## Business Problem

HR leadership wants to reduce employee attrition from approximately 18% to below 12%. The analysis focuses on identifying high-risk employee groups and understanding the major drivers of attrition.

## Dataset

IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset

Records: 1,470 Employees

## Tools Used

* Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
* SQL (MySQL)
* Power BI

## Project Workflow

### 1. Python EDA

Performed exploratory data analysis to identify:

* Salary differences between employees who stayed and left
* Age and tenure distribution
* Job satisfaction patterns
* Overtime impact
* Correlation between employee attributes

### 2. SQL Analysis

Analyzed:

* Overall attrition rate
* Attrition by department
* Attrition by job role
* Attrition by salary band
* Attrition by overtime
* Attrition by age group
* Attrition by business travel
* Attrition by work-life balance
* Tenure cohort analysis

### 3. Power BI Dashboard

Built a 3-page interactive dashboard:

#### Executive Overview

* KPI Cards
* Department Analysis
* Job Role Analysis
* Salary Band Analysis
* Overtime Analysis

#### Risk Analysis

* Age Group Risk
* Business Travel Risk
* Job Satisfaction Risk
* Work-Life Balance Risk

#### Employee Lifecycle Analysis

* Tenure Analysis
* Promotion Analysis
* Manager Experience Analysis
* Training Analysis

## Advanced Power BI Features

* Drill Through Analysis
* Dynamic Titles
* Navigation Buttons
* Tooltips
* Row Level Security (RLS)

## Key Findings

* Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12%
* Sales Department shows highest attrition.
* Sales Representatives and Laboratory Technicians show highest attrition.
* Employees working overtime exhibit significantly higher attrition.
* Lower salary employees are more likely to leave.
* Frequent business travelers have higher attrition.
* Employees under 30 years old represent the highest-risk age group.
* Low job satisfaction and poor work-life balance are major attrition drivers.
